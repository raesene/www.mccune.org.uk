Great ideas and research deserve great presentation, so this talk is designed to help you present your next set of ideas well!

Have you ever thought about giving a talk at a conference or meet-up, but haven't been sure where to start? Are there things you wish you'd known before getting up on stage for the first time? This talk will answer those questions for you giving you the information you need to get started speaking at meet-ups and conferences. 

Based on 17 years of conference presentation delivery, I'll be sharing what worked for me and also what didn't, and providing some practical examples of how you can help ensure your first or next talk is a success.

This talk was delivered at Steelcon 2026. There's a video [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jBSANnzGjHE) and a companion website with some more info [here](https://tagt.mccune.org.uk/)
